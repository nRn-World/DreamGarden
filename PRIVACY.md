# Privacy Policy — Dream Garden

**Last updated: 24 October 2026**
**Publisher: nRnWorld — bynrnworld@gmail.com**

Dream Garden is a single-player, offline game. This policy describes the build in this repository.

## What the game collects

**Nothing.** The game does not create an account, does not send gameplay data to a server, and does
not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. There are no third-party
scripts, cookies, fingerprints or device identifiers.

## What is stored on your device

The game saves the following locally in your browser or app container:

- your garden, flowers and inventory
- dew, stardust, level, XP and spirit bonds
- story progress and achievements
- language, sound, haptics, motion and text-size settings
- the time of your last visit and daily gift

This data is required for the game to work and never leaves your device through the game. You can
export it to a JSON file, import it, or erase it at any time in **Journal → Settings**. Uninstalling
the app or clearing site data also erases it.

## Network access

The game can be installed as a PWA. After the first load, its service worker serves the game from
your device, so it works with no connection. Opening or updating the game from the web uses your
browser's normal request to the host serving the files; the publisher does not add tracking to
those requests and the static host may keep standard server logs under its own policy.

## Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect any
personal information from anyone, including children.

## Advertising and purchases

This repository's build contains **no advertising and no in-app purchases**. If a future version
adds them, it will ask for consent where required, link to updated privacy terms, and provide a way
to withdraw consent. Store data-safety disclosures must be updated at the same time.

## Your rights

Because the publisher receives no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on a
server. You control all local data through the in-game settings. For questions, contact
bynrnworld@gmail.com.

## Changes

Material changes will be dated and shipped with a new version. Continued use after an update means
you accept the updated policy.
